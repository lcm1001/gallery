A Journey to Lake Como

A Journey to Lake Como · Oil on canvas by Li Chen Ma

Two paintings born from one unforgettable stay · Oil on canvas by Li Chen Ma

"Some places don't just leave an impression — they leave a longing. Lake Como was that place for me. I knew I had to paint it before the feeling faded."

— Li Chen Ma

During our stay at one of Lake Como's historic lakeside hotels — One morning, we took a walk along the lake. On the way back, I saw a lovely little house by the lake. The lake view, the mountains and this little house blended perfectly together, with flowerpots and plants adding a touch of elegance, creating a stunning scene.

This Paiting captures the beauty of the lake and mountains in the most serene way - the lovely little house, flower pots, and plants make this scene showcase the most beautiful natural scenery.

I photographed every corner, knowing I would need those images later, back in the studio, when the light and the fragrance and the absolute stillness of that morning were only a memory.

Hung in a living room or study, especially through the cooler months, it does what the best interiors art does: it makes a space feel inhabited by a life fully lived.
